# HBO Max

Open the episode on `play.hbomax.com`. Everyone in the room needs their own HBO
Max subscription and signs in with their own account; the extension never
handles your sign-in.

- **The room stays on one episode until the leader moves on.** HBO Max starts
  the next episode about 20 seconds before this one ends unless you press
  its own **Cancel autoplay** button. For everyone except the leader, the
  extension presses that button for you and hides the countdown, so nobody is
  carried ahead on their own. When the leader goes to the next episode,
  everyone follows.
- **Plans with ads drift during a break.** Ad breaks land at different points
  for each viewer, and the room re-converges after them; see
  [ad-supported plans](/docs/watch-together#ad-supported-plans).
- **If your copies of an episode differ in length**, because some regions put
  extras in front of it, the participant rail offers to skip them; see
  [when copies differ](/docs/watch-together/when-copies-differ).
