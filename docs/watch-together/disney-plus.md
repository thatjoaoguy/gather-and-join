# Disney+

Open the episode or film on `disneyplus.com`. Everyone in the room needs their
own Disney+ subscription and signs in with their own account; the extension
never handles your sign-in.

- **Only the episode's own player is synced.** The trailers that play on
  Disney+'s home and details pages are left alone, and the participant rail
  appears once you are on the episode itself.
- **The room stays on one episode until the leader moves on.** Disney+ starts
  a short countdown to the next episode once the credits begin. For everyone
  except the leader, the extension holds that countdown back, so nobody is
  carried ahead on their own. When the leader goes to the next episode,
  everyone follows. If Disney+ ever does carry you ahead, the participant rail
  offers the way back.
- **Plans with ads drift during a break.** Ad breaks land at different points
  for each viewer, and the room re-converges after them; see
  [ad-supported plans](/docs/watch-together#ad-supported-plans).
- **If your copies of an episode differ in length**, the participant rail
  offers to skip the extra at the start of the longer one; see
  [when copies differ](/docs/watch-together/when-copies-differ).
