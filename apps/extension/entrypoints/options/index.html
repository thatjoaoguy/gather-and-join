A few things before movie night.

Your camera is optional. Headphones help everyone hear the conversation clearly.

1Microphone

Let your friends hear you. Chrome asks once; the room reuses your answer.

2Camera

Say hello face to face, if you like. Off until you turn it on in a room.

3Connection address

Use the address shared by the person hosting your server. You only need to set this once unless their address changes.

No address yet? One person in the group hosts the server — how to host one.

Lower the show’s volume while I speak

Off by default. With speakers, the show’s own audio can trigger this too.

Diagnostics

If a party went wrong, copy this and send it to whoever runs your server. It is the last few hundred things the extension did: room events, connection states, drift corrections. Nothing is sent anywhere by itself.
